Claim 5 · Eq. 8 JS-divergence loss reproduced (mechanism) Open details ↗

L_JS(x,y) = y·JS(z_c,z_n) + (1−y)·(1−JS(z_c,z_n))
y=1 genuine → loss minimized as JS→0 (align). y=0 spoof → loss minimized as JS→1 (separate). Confirmed by direct unit test on synthetic embeddings. Novelty ("first use") claim: unverifiable by search, left open.
